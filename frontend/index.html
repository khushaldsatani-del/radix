Our Story

Why "Radix"?

Radix was founded in 2026 in Chemnitz by eight students in the Planspiel Web Engineering at Chemnitz University of Technology.

Radix is Latin for root. In maths, the radical sign √ asks for the root of a number. Our logo joins both: an R whose stem grows circuit roots, next to a √. It's a reminder to understand the fundamentals before building on top of them.
